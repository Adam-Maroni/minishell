This FT is called in main $ processing, it establishes the presence of multiple dollars in the word *ar. If multiple $ are found, an alt of the word is created in which spaces are inserted before those $. After that, alt is split on 32 and the different sub-words are one after another sent to the replace FT to become what their variable correspond to. Finally, ar is free'd to become a dup of alt.
